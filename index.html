Formicide

Black colony vs red colony

Gather food, grow your colony, evolve stronger ants, and tear down the red Queen's Nest before her armies reach yours.

How to play

Touch

  • Tap an ant to select it. Double-tap to select every ant of that kind on screen.
  • With ants selected, tap ground to move, food to gather, or an enemy to attack.
  • Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, tap the minimap to jump. Use ⬚ Box to drag-select a group.

Mouse & keyboard

  • Left-click or drag a box to select. Right-click to command. Right-drag or arrow keys to scroll, wheel to zoom.
  • A attack-move, S stop, B build menu (then 1–5), Q W E R T for a structure's buttons, H home, P pause, Esc cancel.

Structures (built by workers)

  • Fungus Garden (100): +15 supply and a slow trickle of food. Supply caps at 100.
  • War Chamber (150): trains Soldiers and Acid Spitters.
  • Acid Tower (125): defensive turret. Needs a War Chamber.
  • Evolution Pit (200): researches upgrades. Needs a War Chamber.
  • Brood Vault (200): trains Juggernauts and Bombardiers. Needs an Evolution Pit.

Ants

  • Worker (50, 1 supply) gathers and builds. Alate Scout (75, 1) is a fast winged flyer with huge vision.
  • Soldier (100, 2) is a melee brawler. Acid Spitter (125, 2) attacks from range.
  • Juggernaut (250, 4) is a giant tank that smashes structures. Bombardier (200, 3) lobs acid that splashes groups.

Upgrades (Evolution Pit)

  • Hardened Carapace +25% fighter health, Serrated Mandibles +25% melee damage, Potent Acid +25% ranged damage and +20% range, Swift Legs +15% speed, Efficient Foraging +50% food per trip.
